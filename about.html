The Architecture of My Career

My Engineering Philosophy

I thrive at the crossroads of electronics, digitalization, and economics. While I love the deep technical challenges of routing custom PCBs and writing embedded code, my ultimate goal is systemic efficiency. I approach every project—from a single sensor module to an enterprise-wide maintenance software rollout—with the same question: How does this add tangible value to the business?

The Dual-Study Crucible (2021-2025)

For four years, I operated as a dual student, balancing the rigorous academic demands of unibz with high-stakes technical project management at Dr. Schär. This was my crucible. It forced me to apply theoretical automation frameworks directly to global production lines. I didn't just study data consolidation and predictive maintenance; I implemented it across international manufacturing plants, managing cross-functional teams and operational budgets.

Current Focus & Future Trajectory

Currently, I have transitioned to full-time study to meticulously finalize my Bachelor of Science. My immediate focus is the execution of my thesis, which explores the integration of Augmented Reality and AI in industrial maintenance. Looking beyond my graduation in July 2026, I plan to pursue a Master's degree to further sharpen my strategic and technological acumen, continuing my mission to architect the automated, data-driven systems of tomorrow.
